Method & limitations

Data

  • Kalshi public API: every settled monthly headline-CPI market (series KXCPI) with hourly bid/ask candles, plus a seeded random sample of the whole exchange for calibration.
  • Cleveland Fed inflation nowcasting archive: the daily nowcast sequence for every month since 2013. No fallback model was needed.
  • ALFRED (St. Louis Fed): first-release CPI, which is exactly what Kalshi settles on (seasonally adjusted CPI-U, one-month change, one decimal, revisions ignored). It matched Kalshi's settlement in every checkable release.
